# Privacy Policy for ManualShelf

**Last updated: November 2026**

Hi,  
I'm Christian, the developer of ManualShelf. Here is, in plain words, how ManualShelf handles your data:

## What I collect as the developer

Nothing. ManualShelf does not send any data to me, and I have no access to your manuals, files or tags.

## Where your manuals are stored

- **On your device:** Manuals, files and tags are stored in the app's database on your device.
- **In your iCloud at Apple:** If an iCloud account is signed in on your device, your manuals are synced automatically between your devices through your private iCloud database. This database belongs to your Apple Account and is operated by Apple; Apple's privacy policy applies to it. You can turn sync off in your device's iCloud settings.

## Loading files from a web address

If you load a file from a web address ("Load from URL"), ManualShelf requests that address directly. The server behind it receives your IP address and the request, as with any download in a browser. Nothing is sent to me.

## Analytics, tracking, advertising

ManualShelf contains no analytics or tracking and shows no advertising.
