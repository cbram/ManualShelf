# App Store Description for ManualShelf

## Description
ManualShelf – your digital manual collection

With ManualShelf, all your user manuals are always at hand – for household appliances, electronics or anything else. Organise, search and view your manuals in one place – and sync them automatically across all your devices with iCloud!

**Features:**
- Clear list of all saved manuals
- Easily add PDF, JPG and PNG files
- Swipe through several images per manual
- Full-screen view for PDFs and images
- **Tags:** Tag manuals and files, filter by tags and edit them later
- **Accessible colour coding:** Tags use colour-blind-safe, high-contrast colours
- **Filter & search:** Filter manuals by title, file name and tags
- **Image rotation & zoom:** Rotate, zoom and pan images
- **Unused tags are removed automatically**
- Sorting and search
- **Automatic iCloud sync between iPhone, iPad and Mac**
- Stored on your device; without a signed-in iCloud account everything stays local
- Modern, adaptive design for iPhone, iPad and Mac
- Dark Mode support
- English and German

**Why ManualShelf?**
- No more paper clutter and long searches!
- Everything digital, everything quick to find.
- Your data stays private – it only syncs through your own iCloud.

## Privacy Policy
**Privacy Policy for ManualShelf**

ManualShelf stores all data on your device.  
If an iCloud account is signed in on your device, your manuals are synced automatically between your devices through your private iCloud database. You can turn this off in your device's iCloud settings.  
The private iCloud database is operated by Apple; Apple's privacy policy applies to it.  
No data is sent to the developer, and the developer has no access to your manuals.  
The app contains no analytics or tracking and shows no advertising.  
If you need support, you can contact the developer through the support URL.  
For further questions about privacy, please contact: [https://github.com/cbram/ManualShelf](https://github.com/cbram/ManualShelf)
